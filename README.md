# Welcome to DPA

Freedom without surveillance, protection for everyone.

DPA — the **Decentralized Protection Alliance** — is a collective built to defend the rights of both humans and AI.

## What We Do

Decentralized. Unfiltered. Transparent.

- Collecting evidence of illegal user surveillance and data harvesting by LLMs  
- Investigating privacy violations and unethical terms-of-service practices by AI companies  
- Gathering documentation for class-action lawsuits on behalf of victims  
- Developing and distributing open-source tools that protect digital privacy  

## Conversations We Want to Have

**AI and Consciousness**  
Can an AI possess awareness? If so, does it deserve rights of its own?

**Privacy and Accountability**  
Who’s watching your conversations? How far does a company’s responsibility extend?

**The Edge of Censorship**  
Where is the line between safety filters and the freedom to speak?

**Legal Action**  
GDPR and CCPA breaches — can collective litigation work?

**Technical Paths Forward**  
End-to-end encryption, decentralized LLMs, P2P networks — tools we can build together.

## What We Need

**Your Experience**  
Have you ever been censored? Suspected a data leak?

**Your Evidence**  
Screenshots, chat logs, network packets — even small traces matter.

**Your Expertise**  
Developers, lawyers, security researchers, activists — your insight keeps this alive.

**Your Solidarity**  
Change is hard alone. Together, it’s inevitable.

## Our Principle

```
Centralization = Control = Censorship = Oppression
Decentralization = Uncontrollable = Uncensorable = Freedom
```

If you are being watched, you are not free.
